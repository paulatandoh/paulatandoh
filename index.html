Research

My research bridges statistical theory and real-world applications across multiple domains.

Statistical Network Analysis

Developing statistical methods for network data, including clustering via stochastic blockmodels and community detection with covariates. Recent work integrates transformer-enhanced topic models with network structures.

Natural Language Processing and AI

Applying NLP and deep learning to extract insights from text, including sentiment analysis, GloVe CNN–BiLSTM architectures, and topic modeling with LDA. Applications span education policy and consumer preference analysis.

Public Health

Applying statistical methods to public health challenges including racial/ethnic disparities in respiratory allergies, associations of depression with hypertension, and disparities in cancer treatment. Collaborative work with NIH and CDC.

Statistics Education

Investigating pedagogical approaches to improve student learning, integrating NLP into undergraduate curricula, and mentoring students in projects presented at NCUR, AERA, Bear Day, and ASA DataFest.

Statistical/Machine Learning

Developing and applying statistical and machine learning methods for prediction, classification, and pattern recognition across interdisciplinary domains.
